Hero Book Builder

Turn your Hero of the Holidays pages into print-ready PDFs. Nothing is uploaded: your pages stay in your browser.

One-click from Venice

  1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar (one time only): Make my Hero Book
  2. When your book is finished in Venice, stay on that chat and click Make my Hero Book in your bookmarks bar.
  3. This page opens with every page already in order, ready to download as PDFs for Lulu.

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac). On a phone, use the manual option below.

Your book

Pages arrive here automatically, or drop your page images hereYou can also click to choose files. Drag tiles to change the order.
Pages are sized to US comic size, 6.625 x 10.25 in, with 0.125 in bleed (6.875 x 10.5 in).

Print it at Lulu

  1. Download the print interior PDF and the cover PDF above.
  2. Go to lulu.com/create/comic-books and create a free account.
  3. Choose Comic Book, 6.63 x 10.25 in, Saddle Stitch, Premium Color, Glossy cover.
  4. Upload the two PDFs and order. About $7 to $10 per copy plus shipping (US mail from about $6).

Holiday tip: allow 2 to 3 weeks with mail shipping. Order by early December, and keep the digital PDF ready to share on Christmas morning.
